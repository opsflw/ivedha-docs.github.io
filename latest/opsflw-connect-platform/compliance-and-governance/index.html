    Compliance and governance

    Opsflw Connect operations follow governance and control practices designed for enterprise-managed services.

    Governance outcomes

    End users can expect:

    • Controlled change management for service-impacting actions
    • Traceable support and operations workflows
    • Access governance through role-based controls
    • Security and operational oversight processes

    Compliance posture

    Opsflw Connect is operated to support common enterprise compliance needs, including controlled access, auditability, and standardized operational procedures.

    Specific regulatory mappings and implementation artifacts are handled through formal customer and compliance channels.

    Your role as a customer

    To maintain compliance alignment, customer teams should:

    • Assign access by business role
    • Keep user and ownership records current
    • Use formal support and change channels
    • Follow internal governance and data handling policies
